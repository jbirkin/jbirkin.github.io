Data & Catalogs

  • Sub-millimetre galaxy CO properties: from my 2021 paper. The following data tables are available -
    • table_a1.txt - Full list of all sources targeted in the paper, including co- ordinates, the subsample they belong to, 870-μm fluxes, photometric, and spectroscopic redshifts. Also includes pre-existing stellar masses, SFRs and dust masses.
    • table_a2.txt - Full table of line properties for sources with CO line detections, along with serendipitously detected CO emitters. Includes the frequency, integrated line flux, redshift, linewidth, and line luminosity of all CO detections from the main sample, along with 3-mm continuum measurements. Also includes MAGPHYS-fitted dust masses, stellar masses, infrared luminosities, SFRs, and ages for the main sample. Ssources that display double-peaked line profiles and which z < 3 sources are classified as AGN according to the Donley et al. (2012) criteria (see Section 2.5 from the paper) are also highlighted.
    • table_a3.txt - Full table of sources used to construct the CO SLED in Section 3.3 of the paper and also those plotted as ‘literature SMGs’ in Fig. 7.
    • table_a4.txt - Table of [CI](3P1−3P0) line properties from the sample, including similar information to table_a2.txt.
  • Resolved fits of SPT0418-47 and SPT2147-50: from my 2023 paper -
    • Data cubes used for both sources, both at the native resolution and convolved to a spatial resolution of 0.5''.
    • Masks and 1-D spectra.
    • H-alpha and [NII] parameter cubes for both sources.
    • Results files in .py format containing the measured integrated properties for both sources.
  • KAOSS survey and H-alpha kinematics:
    • Data cubes for N sources.

Analysis tools

Both are documented in their READMEs, with worked examples. The same repositories are listed, framed as software rather than as research tools, on the code page.

  • cubespecfit — spaxel-by-spaxel emission-line fitting for integral-field and interferometric data cubes. Fits a chosen line model pixel by pixel, with adaptive spatial binning to hold signal-to-noise up in the faint outskirts, and produces diagnostic maps so you can see where a fit has gone wrong. Used for the metallicity maps in Birkin et al. (2023) and the kinematics in Birkin et al. (2024).
  • alma-stacking-pipeline — aligns, normalizes and stacks low-S/N spectra across a sample to bring out features too weak to detect in any single source. Includes MCMC line fitting, dropout filtering, bootstrap error estimation and catalog utilities. Used for the [CII] outflow stack in Birkin et al. (2025).

Interactive apps

  • image-viewer-dash — a Dash/Python application for stepping through multi-wavelength cutouts of SPT3G sources alongside MeerKAT and SPIRE imaging, so a distributed team can inspect and annotate candidates in one place instead of passing files around. Deployed on AWS during the survey; the repository includes what you need to run it locally against your own catalog.
