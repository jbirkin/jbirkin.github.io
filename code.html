Code

Below, I will provide links to code I have developed that I believe will be valuable tools for fellow astronomers. I am committed to creating practical, reliable, and innovative solutions to common challenges in the field. By sharing these tools, I aim to contribute to the broader astronomical community and support collaborative progress in research and data analysis.

Analysis and modeling of multidimensional datasets

In the modern era of data-driven research, resolving the internal structures of distant galaxies is becoming increasingly feasible and essential for understanding their properties. My work involves analyzing complex, multidimensional datasets that combine spatial and spectral information, effectively creating 3D maps of these galaxies. To facilitate this analysis, I developed a custom tool for modeling emission features on a pixel-by-pixel basis, enabling more detailed insights into the data.

Signal normalization and aggregation

A flexible pipeline for stacking and modeling [CII] spectra from multiple high-redshift galaxies. Includes MCMC model fitting, dropout filtering, bootstrap error estimation, and catalog utilities.
